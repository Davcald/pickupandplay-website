About Pickup & Play

A free community-driven platform that helps you discover and join local pickup sports games.

Local Sports

Find games happening right in your neighborhood, organized by other sports enthusiasts like you.

All Skill Levels

Whether you're a beginner or advanced player, there's a game for everyone in our inclusive community.

Completely Free

Pickup & Play is free to use with no subscription fees or hidden costs ever.

Features

Everything you need to find, join and organize local sports games.

Create Events

Easily schedule games and events with our intuitive interface. Set location, time, and player limits in seconds.

Join Games

Find local games that match your interests and join with a single click. Filter by sport, location and time.

Real-Time Updates

Receive instant notifications about any event changes or new games nearby that match your preferences.
